# What is the meaning of life?

I love cats, but you'll quickly notice some patterns in their behaviour, and you might say "you can't predict them," and that is correct from a human perspective. Humans don't have all the same sensory inputs as cats. But just because a human cannot predict a cat doesn't mean that cats can't be predicted at all. For one, you can still make guesses and be correct many times.

And the only way in which humans really differ from other animals is in intelligence; there's not something fundamentally different. It's also not like all life experience and everything ever seen or heard is a consideration in every decision made; there's maybe a few dozen things that make a person who they are and that guide their decisions. If you could influence those, you could control them. If you could model those, you could predict them.

So, we live in a deterministic universe with no free will, but that's no reason to be angry or afraid. Life is beautiful because it is fleeting, and because you could say it's the universe admiring itself. What's more; all life has an engrained desire *to work towards a better tomorrow*. For their family, for their kin. Because we're all in this together. And that's exactly what you should do; no, it's exactly what you will do.
