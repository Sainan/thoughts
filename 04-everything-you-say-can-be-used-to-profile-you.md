# Everything you say can be used to profile you

When someone says "I wish cars were cheaper," there is a very implicit context of them looking at car prices and *at least* considering buying.

This is "reading between the lines," or at least one way of doing so, and it can be quite subjective; some people will always read sentences with this kind of perspective of "there must be a reason why you said it."

Let's take a different example: "I think weed should be legal." If a politician said it, maybe they just want more voters? Or maybe this person has some drug-related secrets? See how even with this harmless sentence can be spinned in a scandalous way.

Now, what can you do about it? Well, I think it helps to just be aware of this; review your own sentences with this kind of perspective, and add clarifying context, e.g. "After reviewing all the evidence, I have come to the conclusion that weed has no negative health effects and if the only reason it's illegal is due to public safety, then the law should be changed."

Or, you know, you could simply avoid talking to disingenuous people/trolls.
