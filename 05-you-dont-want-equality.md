# You don't want equality.

Okay, [my last thought](04-everything-you-say-can-be-used-to-profile-you.md) ended on "you could simply avoid talking to disingenuous people/trolls" but, really, what choice do you have if you fall under the LGBTQ umbrella?

You say "equality" but I simply don't know what you mean; you already have equal rights. Gay people can get married, by which *I* mean a union between a man and a woman. If that's not enough for you, then you don't want equal rights, you want to have some weird, special rights!

So, really, arguing about the semantics of what constitutes "equal rights" is simply not even a useful framing. Instead, we should seek to establish "basic human rights," and I think agency should be high and bolded on that list.

Another example is trans people, who have a medically recognised disorder known as "gender dysphoria." Currently, the best known treatment for it is to have medical interventions, such as hormone replacement therapy, to make the body more like the mind's self-image. Now, objectively, these are all "special needs" and we are very lucky that nowadays we have the science and medicine to meet these needs. But under the "equality" framing, it's an incredibly hard sell. A "normal person" doesn't need all this medical help.

But okay, let's say you pay for everything out of your own pocket; you're not leeching off of the social state, but still, you're exercising agency, which is not a basic human right. I mean, what if you're making a bad decision? We just care so much about you that we can't let you do it. Really, it's for your own good.
